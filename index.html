What Are Locomotor Skills?

Locomotor skills are movements that allow the body to travel from one place to another.

Moving With Purpose

Locomotor skills are important fundamental movements used in sports, games, exercise, and everyday activities.

These movements help develop coordination, balance, strength, agility, speed, and body control.

FUNDAMENTAL MOVEMENTS

Basic Locomotor Skills

Explore eight fundamental movements used in physical activities and sports.

01

Walking

Walking is a basic movement where one foot moves forward at a time while maintaining balance and control.

02

Running

Running is a faster locomotor movement in which both feet briefly leave the ground.

03

Jumping

Jumping involves pushing off the ground with one or both feet and landing safely.

04

Hopping

Hopping is moving upward and forward using one foot while maintaining balance.

05

Skipping

Skipping combines a step and a hop while alternating between the feet.

06

Leaping

Leaping involves taking off from one foot and landing on the opposite foot.

07

Galloping

Galloping uses a forward step followed by the trailing foot while maintaining rhythm.

08

Sliding

Sliding is a sideways movement where one foot leads while the other follows.

BODY CONTROL

Balance and Stability

Balance allows us to control our body while performing different movements.

Why Is Balance Important?

Balance helps us maintain body control while standing, walking, running, jumping, and performing sports activities.

  • Posture Maintain proper body position.
  • Coordination Control different body parts together.
  • Stability Stay controlled while moving.
  • Confidence Perform physical activities safely.

POWER & EXPLOSIVENESS

Plyometric Exercises

Plyometric exercises use quick and powerful movements to improve strength, speed, and power.

01

Squat Jumps

A squat followed by an explosive jump that develops lower-body power.

02

Broad Jumps

A powerful forward jump that develops explosive strength and coordination.

03

Jumping Lunges

A dynamic lunge movement that improves leg strength, balance, and power.

04

Step-Up Jumps

An explosive movement using a raised platform to develop lower-body power.

FINAL THOUGHT

Move Better. Move Stronger.

Locomotor skills form the foundation of many physical activities. Practicing these movements helps improve coordination, balance, agility, strength, and confidence.

Developing these basic skills can help prepare individuals for more advanced physical activities and sports.
